# EthniCity
The modern workplace is getting increasingly diverse, including individuals from various
ethnic backgrounds, values, and customs. Organisations looking to create inclusive and
well-being-enhancing environments will face both possibilities and difficulties in this diverse
cultural milieu. Developing a comprehensive plan that prioritises employees' well-being and
acknowledges and celebrates their diverse cultural origins is essential to resolving these
difficulties.
The seriousness of these problems is illustrated by notable examples involving big
businesses. Google faced criticism for fostering a hostile work environment by making
derogatory comments about a worker's cultural customs, which caused the worker to feel
alienated (Smith, 2024). Overwork at Amazon has resulted in staff burnout, which has an
impact on their general well-being and productivity (Jones, 2024). These instances
demonstrate how urgently it is necessary to make noticeable and prompt efforts to increase
workplace well-being and cultural awareness.
In this attempt, we offer an innovative AI-driven solution - EthniCity to these urgent
problems. Our robust artificial intelligence analyses diagnostic data and employee
interactions to find areas that could benefit from improvement as well as threats to well-being
and cultural diversity. With the help of technology, managers and staff can take proactive
measures and establish a healthy work environment because it provides instantaneous and
valuable information.
In line with moral business principles, our approach places a high priority on employee
wellbeing and cultural diversity. Creating a work environment where everyone feels
appreciated and supported is the aim of promoting inclusion and respect. 
Main Function
Cultural and Culinary Awareness Hub
Holiday Descriptions and Customs: Provides information on different international
celebrations, customs, and appropriate levels of workspace custom recognition.
Culinary Cultural Guide: Has an AI Advanced Dietary Database for dietary requirements
and limitations, a Global Recipe Exchange for sharing global recipes and an Inclusive
Corporate Meal Planner using artificial intelligence with corporate menus.
Upgrade Function
Real-Time Cultural Alerts
Contextual Reminders: Sends reminders about culture-related activities and events based
on the calendar notes, participants in meetings and location, thereby increasing the
personnel’s consideration of cultural values.
Situational Prompts: Offers AI-generated advice on the cultural background of the meeting
participants for the proper language use.
Support System
Self-Learning and Continuous Improvement
Automated Data Analysis: Optimises algorithm and adapts content suggestion based on
data collected from the users to determine relevance and correctness.
Feedback and Reflection Integration: Analyse employee feedback through natural
language processing for the system's future modifications.
Adaptive Continuous Learning Engine: Updates system database timely to reflect the real
and live change within the society.
Mock Situation System Prompt
Case: EthniCity’s Support to a New Employee with a Chinese Background
A new employee from the Han ethnic group in China joined the company. On registration,
they fill in their cultural choices in the preferences and determine their keen interest in
Chinese New Year and culture, and their culturally acceptable practices and foods during the
celebration.
Cultural and Culinary Awareness Hub Activation:
● Prompt cultural session for employee system to present information on the Chinese
New Year celebration and its functions.
● Generate special menus recommended for the cafeteria around New Year, for
example, dumplings and fish.
● Propose decoration ideas and activity plans for an upcoming party (optional
depending on the company resource and schedule).
Real-Time Cultural Alerts:
● Notify employees about the Chinese New Year approaching with holiday details and
suggested greetings.
● Provide subtle cultural cues during discussions related to the holiday.
